I have been working as a literary translator for over fifteen years, specialising in fiction and philosophy. Among the authors I have translated are Hermann Hesse, Patrick Süskind, Siegfried Lenz, Bertha von Suttner, Hugo von Hofmannsthal, Norman Davies, W.&nbsp;G.&nbsp;Sebald, Lutz Seiler, and Kim de l’Horizon. In the field of philosophy, I have translated works by Friedrich Nietzsche, Theodor W.&nbsp;Adorno, Byung-Chul Han, Günther Anders, and Frédéric Gros.

Franz Kafka holds a special place in my translation work. From his writings I have translated into Catalan [_Letter to His Father_](https://lafinestralectora.cat/carta-al-pare/), the short story collection [_Parables and Paradoxes_](https://www.editorialflaneur.cat/ca/llibre/paraboles-i-paradoxes/), [_The Trial_](https://www.editorialflaneur.cat/ca/llibre/paraboles-i-paradoxes/)(https://clubeditor.cat/llibres/el-proces/), and [_The Castle_](https://clubeditor.cat/llibres/el-castell/) — the last of which received the 5th Montserrat Franquesa Translation Prize in 2020. In 2024, my translation of Leyb Rokhman’s [_Mit blinde trit iber der erd_](https://clubeditor.cat/llibres/a-passes-cegues-per-la-terra/) der erd was published, marking the first novel ever translated directly from Yiddish into Catalan.
